# Artifact Explorer

A reference for everything artifacts: effects by rarity, crafting recipes, ingredient trees, and mission drop rates.

> Read the root [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) first — Artifact, Family, Tier, Rarity, Slot, Stone, Crafting, Mission, Quality, and Loot are defined there. This file adds only Artifact Explorer terms.

## Language

**Ingredient**:
A crafted intermediate item consumed to build a higher-tier artifact or stone; not equippable itself.
_Avoid_: component, part, material

**Recipe**:
The formula for crafting an item — the ingredients and quantities required, plus the Golden Egg cost and crafting XP.
_Avoid_: blueprint, formula

**Recursive ingredient**:
An ingredient that recurs several levels deep in a crafting chain (ingredients of ingredients); the full material sink for an item.

**Hard dependency**:
The lowest-level mission-obtainable items that must be farmed to complete an item's full ingredient tree (and, conversely, the unobtainable artifacts that depend on a given drop).

**Base quality**:
An artifact tier's inherent quality, which determines the missions whose Quality range can drop it.

**Odds multiplier**:
A per-tier factor scaling how often that tier drops relative to others in the same mission and quality band.

**Drop rate**:
The observed frequency a tier appears in mission loot, from user-submitted data, expressed per-mission and per-day.

**Target**:
An artifact a Faster-Than-Light mission is specifically tuned to drop, as opposed to incidental untargeted drops.
_Avoid_: goal

**Value**:
A per-tier usefulness score, distinct from crafting price.
