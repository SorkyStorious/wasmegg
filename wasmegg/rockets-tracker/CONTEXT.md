# Rockets Tracker

Tracks active missions, aggregates historical mission statistics, and visualizes artifact and fuel collection progress.

> Read the root [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) first — Mission, Spaceship, Launch Point, Sensor, Quality, Fuel, Loot, Permit, Artifact, and Crafting are defined there. This file adds only Rockets Tracker terms.

## Language

**Ship level**:
A spaceship's progression milestone, reached as accumulated Launch Points cross thresholds; each level grants bonuses.
_Avoid_: rank, stars (stars is the UI display of ship level)

**Active mission**:
A launched mission that has not yet returned, as opposed to one in the Mission archive.

**Mission archive**:
The historical record of completed missions.

**Artifact club**:
A membership badge based on legendary-collection status — e.g. Zero Legendary Club (no legendaries), All Legendaries Club (39+ distinct), Staff Legendaries Club.

**Zero Legendary Club**:
The badge for players with no legendary artifacts; sub-tiers include ZLC 100 (100 extended Henerprise missions) and ZLC 7-Star (a maxed Henerprise with zero legendaries).
_Avoid_: ZLC (in prose)

**All Star Club**:
The badge for players who have maxed every spaceship.

**Virtue inventory**:
A separate artifact pool from virtue rocket missions, tracked apart from the standard inventory.

**Crafting expenses**:
Cumulative Golden Eggs spent on crafting, estimated from the backup.
