# Researches

A structured, SQL-queryable database of every common and epic research and its effect.

> Read the root [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) first — Common Research and Epic Research are defined there. This file adds only the terms describing how a research's effect is modeled.

## Language

**Effect type**:
Whether a research's effect is additive or multiplicative on its target game mechanic.
_Avoid_: operation, mode

**Per level**:
The numerical bonus a research grants for each level purchased.
_Avoid_: increment, step

**Levels compound**:
How successive levels of one research combine — additively or multiplicatively.
_Avoid_: stacking
