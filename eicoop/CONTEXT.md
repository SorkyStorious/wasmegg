# eicoop (CoopTracker)

A co-op and solo contract status tracker: live progress on contract farms, contributor analysis, and saved dashboards.

> Read the root [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md) first — Contract, Co-op, Contract Season, Leggacy, Grade, League, and Earning Bonus are defined there. This file adds only eicoop-specific terms.

## Language

**Contributor**:
A player participating in a Co-op contract, listed with their per-player stats.
_Avoid_: member, participant

**Projected eggs laid**:
A farm's estimated total eggs laid by contract expiry — current eggs plus expected additional eggs at the current lay rate.

**Offline eggs**:
Eggs estimated to have accumulated while a contributor's game was closed, capped at 30 hours and only when farm data is shared.

**Soul mirror multiplier**:
A normalizing factor that scales a contributor's performance relative to the highest-Earning-Bonus player in the co-op, for fair comparison.

**Finalized**:
A contributor's state once they've stopped contributing, typically after the co-op hits its final goal.

**Ghost coop**:
A co-op with an empty roster — no contributors.

**Leech**:
A contributor flagged as taking co-op rewards while contributing little.
_Avoid_: freeloader, parasite

**Time cheat**:
A flag for a contributor whose timestamps indicate device-clock manipulation.
_Avoid_: time travel, clock hack
